# Lesson 4 — Logits, Temperature, Top-K & Top-P

Now we move into **LLM inference**: how a model goes from its internal representation to the actual next token.

This is important because parameters such as `temperature`, `top_k`, and `top_p` are not magic settings. They modify the model's **token-selection process**.

---

## 1. Today's Objective

You will understand:

* Logits
* Probability distributions
* Temperature
* Top-K sampling
* Top-P / nucleus sampling
* Greedy decoding
* Randomness vs determinism
* Why LLM answers can change
* How these settings affect your Ollama applications

# 11. Top-K vs Top-P


| Setting     | Mechanism                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Temperature | Changes probability distribution                 |
| Top-K       | Keeps K highest-probability candidates           |
| Top-P       | Keeps candidates within cumulative probability P |
| Greedy      | Selects highest-probability token                |

These can also be combined.
